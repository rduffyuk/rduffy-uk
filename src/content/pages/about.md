<header class="about-hero"><p class="section-label">About Ryan</p><h1>Reliable systems.<br>Curious by nature.</h1><p class="lead">I’m Ryan Duffy. I work in site reliability at BT and explore applied AI through Rootweaver, an independent research platform I develop and operate.</p><p class="hero-note">Based in Ipswich, UK.</p><a class="button" href="/cv/">View my CV</a><a class="text-link" href="/writing/">Read my engineering notes</a></header>
<section class="editorial"><h2>From networks<br>to AI systems</h2><div><p>My background is in keeping networks and services working: tracing faults across distributed systems, restoring service and understanding why things failed.</p><p>That experience shapes the questions I bring to AI. What evidence should we trust? How do we know a retrieval result is useful? What happens when an agent gets something wrong?</p><p>Rootweaver is where I investigate those questions. It gives me a place to design experiments, connect systems and examine their behaviour over time.</p></div></section>
<section class="editorial"><h2>How I work</h2><div><p>I use AI agents extensively for implementation. I own the problem selection, architecture, controls, acceptance criteria, verification and operational outcome.</p><p>For me, the interesting work is turning an idea into something that can be tested, challenged and understood.</p><dl class="principles"><div><dt>Start with a question</dt><dd>Define what we need to learn and what evidence would change the decision.</dd></div><div><dt>Make behaviour inspectable</dt><dd>Keep decisions, observations and uncertainty visible.</dd></div><div><dt>Learn from operation</dt><dd>Use failures and measured results to inform the next change.</dd></div></dl></div></section>
